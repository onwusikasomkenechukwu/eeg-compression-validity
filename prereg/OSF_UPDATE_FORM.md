## Page 6: Analysis Plan → Inference criteria (add at the end)

> Update (registered after Stage 0 ran). The FP32 sanity gate above failed (voted window FP/h 5.83) and is reported as failed. Added: a protocol-matched reimplementation check, FP32 only, 3 seeds. For every CHB-MIT patient, leave one seizure record out as EpiDeNet describes: train on all other seizure-containing records (seizure-free records unused; same channels, preprocessing, architecture and training windows as Stage 0), test on the excluded record, positive window = the model's predicted class (probability > 0.5, no threshold tuning), EpiDeNet's 2-of-3 vote on 4 s windows. Early stopping (no validation fold in EpiDeNet's protocol): a random 25% of the training windows, stratified by class and drawn with the job's seed, held out from gradient updates; patience 10 epochs, at most 300, best epoch kept. Pass criteria: the gate's criteria above, unchanged. Decision rule: if the check passes, the reimplementation is taken to reproduce EpiDeNet and EpiDeNet stays the primary model for later stages; if it fails, Table I is rechecked against the code, and if still off, Ahlawat's model becomes primary for later stages. Either way the result is reported with the failed gate and the investigation.

## Page 6: Analysis Plan → Exploratory analysis (add at the end)

> Update (registered after Stage 0 ran). Because the registered gate failed and Stage 0's results were seen before this update, all Stage 0 results, including the primary endpoint and the stop rule, are reported as exploratory, as registered but labelled so, whatever the reimplementation check shows. Added (exploratory): sensitivity under a false-alarm cap, computed from Stage 0's saved frontiers with no new training. For each patient, seed and model: SzCORE event sensitivity at the smoothing width with the most detected seizures among those whose false alarms per 24 h are at or below the cap (ties: the smaller width; no interpolation). Caps: 0.5, 1 and 2 false alarms per 24 h. Reported per model: pooled sensitivity (seizures detected over seizures, summed over patients where the model reaches the cap, per seed, then the median over seeds); paired change vs FP32 (per patient, the median over seeds where both reach the cap of the model's sensitivity minus the same seed's FP32, then the median over patients with a 95% patient-bootstrap interval); and the number of patient-seed values where the model cannot reach the cap (reported, not imputed). Bit widths: INT8 is the deployed case (native on Cortex-M microcontrollers with CMSIS-NN and on GAP8/GAP9). Simulated 6- and 4-bit quantize both weights and activations; mainstream microcontrollers have no native arithmetic below 8 bits, so these are reported as stress points that show where the trade-off starts to move, not as deployment scenarios.
