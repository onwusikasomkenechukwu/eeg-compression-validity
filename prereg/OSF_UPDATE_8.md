## Justification tab

> Eighth update. The advisor asked for a second detector family: both models tested so far (EpiDeNet, Ahlawat's CNN) are small 1D/2D convolutional networks. This update registers a test of H1 with EEGformer (Busia et al., IEEE TBioCAS 18(3):608-621, 2024, doi:10.1109/TBCAS.2024.3357509), a compact transformer for wearable EEG on microcontrollers whose authors deploy it in 8-bit integers, before any of its results exist. EEGformer has no public code; it is reimplemented from the paper's Table I, as EpiDeNet was, and three fidelity checks are registered below. Disclosed: during development the pipeline was tested on the laptop with 2-epoch smoke runs on real data (chb02, fold 0, seed 0; main design and check C); their logs and one 2-epoch check-C output were seen. A 2-epoch model is not a result, and none of these runs is used. The Ahlawat result (H1 not supported, updates 2-3) and the Siena replication (exploratory, updates 6-7) are unchanged.

## Page 5: Variables → Manipulated variables (add at the end)

> Update 8. Model: EEGformer as in Table I of Busia et al. (2024): two stride-5 convolutions (4 -> 32 -> 32 channels) embedding 8 s windows (2048 samples at 256 Hz) into 81 tokens, a learned positional encoding, one pre-norm encoder block (multi-head attention with 8 heads and query/key/value size 32; feed-forward network 32 -> 128 -> 32 with GELU after both dense layers; residual connections; final LayerNorm), mean pooling and a dense layer to 2 logits; 50,594 parameters (paper: 50.6 K). Input: the four temporal bipolar channels F7-T7, T7-P7, F8-T8, T8-P8, with this study's preprocessing (causal 0.5-40 Hz band-pass, 256 Hz, standardization from training windows). Training as in the paper's two-phase strategy: (1) subject-independent pre-training on the seizure records of every other CHB-MIT patient (never the test patient; chb01 and chb21 are one person and excluded together), at most 100 epochs, with a stratified random 20% of the pre-training windows held out to keep the best epoch; (2) subject-specific fine-tuning from those weights inside this study's folds, at most 50 epochs, the validation fold choosing the best epoch. Both phases: Adam, learning rate 5e-5, batch 16 (the paper's settings), class-weighted cross-entropy and this study's training windows; early stopping after 20 epochs without improvement (a compute bound the paper does not state). One pre-training per patient and seed, shared by that patient's folds. Precisions as registered for the other models: FP32; post-training INT8 (ONNX Runtime static, QDQ; primary); simulated 8-, 6- and 4-bit; quantization-aware 8-bit (fine-tuned from the job's FP32 model, batch 16); exploratory W4A8 and FP16. In the simulated paths, both operands of the two attention products (query-key scores and attention-weighted values) are quantized like a layer input; LayerNorm, softmax and GELU stay in float there. In the ONNX INT8 model, Conv, MatMul and Gemm run on INT8 kernels and the other operators read INT8-rounded inputs except GELU's error function (check A lists every operator); the authors' microcontroller runs all of them in integer arithmetic, which this test does not reproduce.

## Page 6: Analysis Plan → Inference criteria (add at the end)

> Update 8: test of H1 with EEGformer.
>
> Design: patient-specific, as registered (each patient's records grouped into folds; fold k tests on fold k, validates on fold k+1, trains on the rest), with every CHB-MIT patient and the four temporal channels: 138 folds, seeds 0-4 (690 fold jobs), plus 120 pre-training jobs (24 patients x 5 seeds). Environment: the GPU workstation of update 3, unchanged (Ubuntu 26.04.1 LTS, Python 3.12.15, torch 2.14.1+cu132 with CUDA 13.2 and cuDNN 9.24.0, NVIDIA driver 595.99.02, 2 x RTX 4090, package versions as in requirements.txt apart from the torch build), code at commit 9ab65fb; training on the GPU with cuDNN's deterministic mode, quantization and scoring on the CPU at 4 threads per job; one runner per GPU, 2 jobs per GPU. Repeatability check before the run, as in update 3: one full job (chb02 pre-training and fold 0, seed 0) run at the same time on both GPUs must give identical saved arrays, compared without inspecting metrics; outputs deleted. Result (2026-11-25): identical (both saved-array files, every array); unit tests (41) and check A passed on the workstation at the same commit.
>
> Fidelity checks. All three must pass for the test to count as confirmatory; otherwise it is reported as exploratory, with the failure. (A) Architecture, checked before the run (done): tensor shapes after every stage as in Table I (Table I prints 405 samples after the first convolution; a stride-5 convolution of 2048 samples gives 409, and both give 81 tokens) and 50,594 parameters against the published 50.6 K; `scripts/check_eegformer.py`. (B) Operating point, as registered for Ahlawat's model: per patient and seed, FP32 test window specificity pooled over all held-out hours within 0.995 +/- 0.005 for at least two thirds of patient-seed values. (C) Protocol-matched check, FP32 only, run first: EEGformer's own CHB-MIT protocol on chb01-chb08 (pre-training on the seizure records of the other seven patients; leave one seizure record out; fine-tuning on the patient's other seizure records with a stratified random 20% held out to keep the best epoch; test windows of 8 s every 2 s; predicted class; majority vote over 3 windows; false positives within 15 min after a seizure's end ignored; consecutive false positives counted as one event), seeds 0-2, pooled over patients and averaged over seeds. Pass rule, mirroring the registered EpiDeNet check (update 1): seizures detected at least 0.455 (the published miss rate of 12/44 doubled) and event false positives per hour within 0.075-0.30 (the published 0.15, factor of 2). Published reference (Table IV): 32/44 seizures, 0.15 FP/h, latency 15.2 s. Choices the paper does not state, fixed here: training windows and loss as in this study; seizure records only in both phases of check C.
>
> Primary test and decision (seeds 0-4): as registered in update 2, applied to EEGformer. Pooled excess latency of post-training INT8 against the FP32 seed-noise null (95% band from all ordered pairs of FP32 seeds within a patient), with patient-bootstrap intervals and the mixed-effects model. H1 is supported for EEGformer if the pooled INT8 excess latency lies above the null band and is at least 2 s; not supported if it lies inside the band or below 2 s.
